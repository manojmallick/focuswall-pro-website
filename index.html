Don't settle for simple toggles. Choose L1 (soft confirmation), L2 (10-second wait delays), L3 (written justifications), L4 (math challenges), or L5 (Hard Mode absolute lock) to match your self-discipline level.

Active Intention Ledger

When you request a site bypass, you must type your justification. Excuses are written to a private local ledger, letting you review distraction spikes and build habits.

Background Auto-Scheduling

Configure customized work boundaries (e.g. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) that run background sweeps via alarms, starting and stopping focus sessions automatically.

Real-time Tab Sweeper

When focus sessions initiate, FocusWall Pro actively queries and intercepts existing distraction tabs in real-time, ensuring instant lockdown.
