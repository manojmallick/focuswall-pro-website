ChatVault Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-23

ChatVault Pro is a local-first Chrome extension for organizing AI chats. Your vault stays on your device.

1. What we collect

ChatVault stores data only on your device using browser extension storage / IndexedDB. This may include chat titles, URLs, tags, and prompt templates you save.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not operate a ChatVault backend that receives your chat content in the free MVP.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your chats for advertising.

3. Permissions

The extension only runs on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini sites to help organize chats you open there. You can uninstall the extension at any time to remove its local data (or clear site/extension data in browser settings).

4. Contact

Privacy questions: admin@learnhubplay.nl or the publisher email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
