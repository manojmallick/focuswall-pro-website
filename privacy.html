Privacy Policy

FocusWall Pro is built on a local-first, zero-knowledge architecture. Your focus habits are your own business.

1. Zero Server Collections

We do not host or operate backend database servers to harvest your data. Your lists of blocked domains, daily goals, written justifications, schedules, and analytics stats are stored locally on your machine.

2. Local & Sync Storage

FocusWall Pro utilizes two browser-level storage models:

  • chrome.storage.local: Holds active session indicators, lock countdown timers, and statistics. This data is private and device-specific.
  • chrome.storage.sync: Syncs your configurations (blocklist domains, timeline schedules, and logs) across devices signed into your personal Chrome profile. This sync is managed entirely by Google; no data is ever transmitted to us.

3. Web Audio Synthesis

Our ambient soundscapes (Binaural Beats, White Noise, Clouds, Ocean Waves) are generated client-side inside your browser using mathematical synthesizers. They require no remote asset requests and work entirely offline.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our offline local-first privacy philosophy, feel free to contact us at admin@learnhubplay.nl.
